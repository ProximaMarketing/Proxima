The point

Make the release show up more than once — without making the artist do all the work.

We batch the familiar formats, vary the angle and publish the agreed posts over the campaign. The mix depends on the release, the material available and the chosen package. No. We guarantee the agreed posts and research time, not views, streams, followers or placements.
