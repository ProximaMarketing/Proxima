An example release campaign

One release.
Three ways in.

Imagine an artist releasing a new single called Your Track Title. Here's how PROXIMA could turn that release into a short sequence of promotional posts.

the hook.Short-form edit · 12–20 seconds (example)

Post one

A clip that leads with the music

What we make: A vertical edit built around a memorable lyric, instrumental moment or drop, using the track and footage supplied by the artist.

Where it goes: PROXIMA's artist-feature and release-focused TikTok account.

Listener's next step: See the artist and track name, then find the release through the artist's profile or by searching the track.

artist behind it.Sound · Story · Track

Post two

Give people a reason to remember you

What we make: A concise introduction to the artist, their sound and the new single, using agreed copy, artwork or footage.

Where it goes: PROXIMA's artist-feature and release-focused TikTok account.

Listener's next step: Discover who made the track and look up the release or artist profile.

03 / RELEASE ANNOUNCEMENTYour Track Title
is out now.Artist name · New single

Post three

Make the release easy to find

What we make: A clear announcement with the artist name, track title, release artwork and a simple listening prompt.

Where it goes: PROXIMA's artist-feature and release-focused TikTok account.

Listener's next step: Search for the track or use the artist's supplied listening link where it is available.

And the second channel? Where it genuinely fits, the track can also inspire a separate music-culture post — for example, a relevant trend, relatable music moment or meme. That would be created for PROXIMA's culture-focused account rather than forcing every release into a trend.

This is an illustrative campaign plan, not a completed client campaign or a promise of results. The exact mix is agreed around the artist and package. These three example formats count as three posts if all are produced and published; they are not extra posts on top of the monthly allowance.

For independent labels: this gives you something specific to assess at release level — the content formats, where they appear and the listener's next step. You can consider one release first, then discuss whether the same approach makes sense across more of your roster.

The process

From first message
to monthly review.

01

Tell us about your music

We look at your sound and goals, then agree the monthly post count, content direction and publishing plan before work starts.

Approved posts go onto the agreed PROXIMA TikTok account or accounts. Your own social accounts stay yours; we do not need your passwords.

Your monthly report lists each post's publication date, content type and link, plus available views, likes, comments and shares. It highlights the strongest-performing content and what could be tried next.

Good questions

Before you
reach out.

Do I need to share my artist account passwords?

No. We publish through PROXIMA’s own channels.

What do I need to provide?

Your music and any footage or artwork you want us to use. We’ll agree the materials before starting.

What does discovery research involve?

We look for relevant music communities, forums and platforms, then test suitable ways to introduce your music. External acceptance and reach are not guaranteed.

Are results guaranteed?

No. We guarantee the agreed posts and research time, not views, streams, followers or placements.

Can I approve the content first?

Yes. Artist-specific promotional edits are shared for review before publication.

What will my monthly report show?

It summarises the posts published and available platform figures, such as views and engagement. It does not promise a particular result.
