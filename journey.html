An example release campaign

One release.
Three ways in.

This is a worked example using placeholder artist and track details. It shows how a single release can become three different posts, each with a clear job and a route for a listener to find the music.

01 / MUSIC CLIPStart with
the hook.Short-form edit · 12–20 seconds (example)

Post one

A clip that gives the release a clear identity

What the viewer sees: The first seconds lead with the strongest supplied lyric, instrumental moment or drop. The edit then shows the artist name and exact track title on screen, with a caption that identifies the release.

00:00–00:02THE HOOKLead with the strongest musical moment.
00:02–00:14THE MOMENTLet the track carry the edit with approved footage or artwork.
00:14–00:18FIND THE RELEASEShow Artist Name · Track Title · Out now.

Example caption: “Artist Name — Track Title. Out now. Find the full release through the link in the profile.” The names and link are replaced with the artist’s real release details before publication.

Where it goes: PROXIMA’s dedicated Artist & Release TikTok account. The approved edit is published from this account, not uploaded to the artist’s personal profile.

How it connects to the release: Where the official TikTok sound is available and suitable, it can be attached. Otherwise the clip uses the approved audio supplied for the edit, while the artist and track are still clearly named. Any listening link supplied by the artist is included where the platform allows it.

Listener’s next step: Visit the artist’s profile or use the supplied listening link. If a direct link is not available, the on-screen artist and track details give them the exact names to search on their streaming service.

02 / ARTIST INTRODUCTIONMeet the
artist behind it.Sound · Story · Track

Post two

Introduce the person behind the track

What we make: A short artist introduction using agreed footage, artwork and copy. It gives a listener context about the sound and makes the featured single part of the introduction.

Where it goes: The same PROXIMA Artist & Release account, so the artist feature and release clip sit in one place.

Listener’s next step: Recognise the artist, visit the profile and find the named release. This is a discovery route, not a promise that every viewer will click through.

03 / RELEASE ANNOUNCEMENTTrack Title
OUT NOW.Artist Name · New Single

Post three

Make the release details unmistakable

What we make: A release announcement showing the artist name, exact track title, approved artwork and a direct “listen now” prompt.

Where it goes: The PROXIMA Artist & Release account, with the same artist and release details used consistently.

Listener’s next step: Use the supplied listening link where available, or search the exact artist and track title. The post makes the destination clear; it cannot make someone listen.

Why two dedicated accounts?

Two channels. Two different jobs.

01 / ARTIST & RELEASE

The direct route to the music

Artist introductions, approved clips and release announcements are published here. Each post names the artist and track, so someone who discovers the clip can identify the release and find the listening destination.

02 / MUSIC CULTURE

The wider music conversation

This separate account carries relevant music-culture posts, trends and humour. A release may inspire a culture post when there is a genuine fit, but PROXIMA will not force every artist into a meme or imply that a culture post guarantees traffic to their song.

This is an illustrative plan, not a completed client campaign or a promise of results. Each post published once on one PROXIMA account counts as one post. Publishing the same creative on both accounts counts as two posts. The monthly allowance is shared across both accounts, not duplicated.

For independent labels: start by assessing one release rather than committing an entire roster. You can judge the creative, accuracy, approval process, publishing consistency and report before deciding whether the approach suits more artists.

Good questions

Before you
reach out.

Do I need to share my artist account passwords?

No. We publish through PROXIMA’s own channels.

What do I need to provide?

Your music and any footage or artwork you want us to use. We’ll agree the materials before starting.

What does discovery research involve?

We look for relevant music communities, forums and platforms, then test suitable ways to introduce your music. External acceptance and reach are not guaranteed.

Are results guaranteed?

No. We guarantee the agreed posts and research time, not views, streams, followers or placements.

Are the packages available to labels?

The monthly packages are designed for individual artists. Labels can enquire about a separate scope, starting with one priority release before considering wider roster work.

What will my monthly report show?

It lists each post’s date, account, content type and link, with available views, likes, comments and shares. Discovery research is documented separately.
