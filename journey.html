The process

From first message
to monthly review.

01

Tell us about your music

Share your artist links, track, release plans and the kind of support you're looking for.

02

Agree the content plan

We look at your sound and goals, then agree the monthly post count, content direction and publishing plan before work starts.

03

Review before it goes live

We send artist-specific edits for your approval before publication. You can check the track, artist details, wording and visuals so the content represents you properly.

04

Publish through PROXIMA

Approved artist-specific posts go onto PROXIMA’s Artist & Release TikTok account. A relevant culture post may go onto the separate Music Culture account. Your own social accounts stay yours; we do not need your passwords.

05

See what went out and how it did

Your monthly report lists each post’s publication date, content type, publishing account and link, plus available views, likes, comments and shares. It highlights the strongest-performing content and what could be tried next.

Good questions

Before you
reach out.

Do I need to share my artist account passwords?

No. We publish through PROXIMA’s own channels.

What do I need to provide?

Your music and any footage or artwork you want us to use. We’ll agree the materials before starting.

What does discovery research involve?

We look for relevant music communities, forums and platforms, then test suitable ways to introduce your music. External acceptance and reach are not guaranteed.

Are results guaranteed?

No. We guarantee the agreed posts and research time, not views, streams, followers or placements.

Are the packages available to labels?

The monthly packages are designed for individual artists. Labels can enquire about a separate scope, starting with one priority release before considering wider roster work.

What will my monthly report show?

It lists each post’s date, account, content type and link, with available views, likes, comments and shares. Discovery research is documented separately.
