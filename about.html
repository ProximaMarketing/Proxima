The Jean White Project

I’m an independent musician behind The Jean White Project. Making music has also meant making decisions around it: when to release, how to tell the story, what content feels natural and how to understand a listener’s response — usually without a large team.

PROXIMA grew from the idea that these decisions should be made together, around the needs and character of each project. I’m building the approach now and inviting independent artists to help shape its first chapter.

PROXIMA is at an early stage and inviting its first artists. The example on the main site explains the approach; it isn’t previous client work.
