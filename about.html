Building The Jean White Project showed me how much work goes into promoting music alongside writing, recording and releasing it.

PROXIMA Marketing grew from that experience. I wanted to make the content and promotional side easier for independent artists to keep up with, without asking them to become full-time content creators as well.

The Jean White Project is my independent music project, not a PROXIMA client campaign. PROXIMA Marketing is a separate service operated by Louis Cadman as a sole trader.
