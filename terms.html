If we believe the service is a suitable fit, we will confirm availability and send the proposed package, scope, start date, payment arrangements and any agreed variations in writing. A service contract is formed only when you accept that written offer and we confirm acceptance. No payment is taken through the enquiry form on this website.

The monthly package prices currently displayed are £119 for Artist Promotion and £229 for Artist Growth. 3. Packages and what is included

Artist Promotion — £119 per month

  • 10 guaranteed promotional posts per service month, shared across the two PROXIMA-managed accounts.
  • Editing and preparation using music, footage and other materials you supply.
  • Captions, hooks and platform-appropriate promotional copy.
  • 2 hours of included discovery research each month, documented separately from the post allowance.
  • Personal content direction, support and a monthly update.

Artist Growth — £229 per month

  • 20 guaranteed promotional posts per service month, shared across the two PROXIMA-managed accounts.
  • Everything in Artist Promotion, including editing, captions and content direction.
  • 5 hours of included discovery research each month, documented separately from the post allowance.
  • Additional research, testing and performance monitoring, with more active next-step recommendations.

How posts are counted: one item published on one PROXIMA account counts as one post. Publishing the same item on both accounts counts as two posts. Your allowance is shared across the Artist & Release and Music Culture accounts; it is not doubled on each account. The account split is agreed according to the content and campaign.

Label enquiries: the published monthly packages are designed for individual artists. Label or roster-wide work is not automatically included in an individual artist package; it will be scoped and priced separately in writing.

Discovery research: this is included time spent researching relevant communities, tailoring approaches and documenting suitable tests. It is not a guaranteed number of submissions, placements, moderator approvals or third-party posts. Research hours are separate from the guaranteed PROXIMA post allowance. Unless agreed otherwise in writing, unused posts and unused research time do not roll over into another service month.

The precise monthly cycle, planned posting cadence and any delivery dates will be confirmed in your written order confirmation. We will agree any requested change to the scope, price or timing with you before proceeding.

What is not included: the packages do not include management or posting on your personal artist accounts, paid advertising spend, guaranteed playlist placements, guaranteed third-party submissions or guaranteed audience results. We will not incur third-party costs without your prior written agreement.
