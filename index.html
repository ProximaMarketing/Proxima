Good questions

Before you
reach out.

How are my posts split between the two accounts?

Your monthly allowance is a combined total across both PROXIMA accounts. The main account focuses on polished artist promotion, features and release content. The second is a more informal music-culture account for memes, trends, relatable musician content and community discovery. We choose the split based on the content and what appears to be connecting. Each publication counts as one post.

Does every package include Reddit and other platforms?

Yes. Artist Promotion includes 2 hours and Artist Growth includes 5 hours of dedicated experimentation each month. This covers researching relevant Reddit communities, music forums and other suitable platforms, then tailoring and testing suitable approaches. This work is additional to your guaranteed PROXIMA posts. We follow community rules, and external submissions are never guaranteed to be accepted.

Do I need a large following?

No. Artists at any stage can apply. We want to understand your music, where you're at and what you're working towards.

Do I need to supply content?

You'll provide your music and usable footage or other agreed materials. PROXIMA will prepare the promotional content, edit it where needed and write the accompanying copy.

Are results guaranteed?

No. We guarantee the agreed number of PROXIMA posts and the work included in your package, not a particular number of views, streams, followers or community approvals.

Will I be dealing with the same person?

Yes. Think of PROXIMA as a personal music marketing assistant: someone in your corner who gets to know your sound, helps with ideas, keeps promotion moving and looks for new opportunities. No faceless agency process or compulsory meetings — just direct, friendly support.
