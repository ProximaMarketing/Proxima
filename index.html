THE BRIEF / FICTIONAL ARTIST

One song. A clearer direction.

The music is ready to be shared, but the artist's visual identity, content ideas and release timing have not been brought together. The goal is to make the campaign feel coherent and manageable — not to post for the sake of posting.

01CONCEPT

Explore the process

Open each stage to see the thinking behind it.

01Find the signal

Start with what is distinctive about the song: its emotional centre, the artist's point of view and the people who may connect with it. Use that to guide the campaign's visual language and message.

OUTPUT / A clear creative direction
02Shape the release

Map a realistic sequence around the release: an introduction, a few meaningful moments before launch, a clear release-day destination and follow-through afterwards. The plan should fit the artist's capacity and calendar.

OUTPUT / A practical campaign timeline
03Make content work together

Develop a small group of content ideas that express the same creative direction in different ways — for example, a story behind a lyric, a performance moment and a visual detail. Avoid forcing the artist into trends that do not fit.

OUTPUT / Repeatable content ideas
04Learn from the response

After publishing, look at relevant signals such as saves, shares, watch-through, replies and clicks where available. Use them to ask a better next question — not to guarantee growth or pretend one post tells the whole story.

OUTPUT / A more informed next step

The Jean White Project

I'm building PROXIMA from the perspective of an independent musician. Through my own music project, The Jean White Project, I've spent time on the creative side of making music — and thinking about how difficult it can be to connect that work with a clear release plan, consistent content and audience development.

PROXIMA grew from the belief that those parts should work together around the artist, rather than feel like separate jobs competing for time and attention.

I'm not presenting a long client roster or promising overnight success. I'm building the approach carefully and inviting independent artists to start a conversation about what would genuinely help them move forward.

Good questions

Before you
reach out.

Is PROXIMA already working with a roster of artists?

PROXIMA is at an early stage and is opening conversations with independent artists. The sample campaign on this site is fictional and demonstrates the intended approach; it is not presented as past client work or a proven result.

Do you work with every genre?

We look for a distinct point of view, not a genre label. Share your music and influences so we can understand the project.

Do I need a large following?

No. Tell us where you are and where you want to go. Fit matters more than follower count alone.

Can you guarantee streams or viral results?

No. We can sharpen your story and plan, but nobody can promise streams, audience behaviour or virality.

What happens after I apply?

We aim to reply within 3–5 business days. If it feels like a fit, we’ll arrange a conversation. Applying is not a commitment.

How does pricing work?

Scope and cost depend on your needs. We’ll agree both before any work begins.
