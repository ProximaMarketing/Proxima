Good questions

Before you
reach out.

A few things worth knowing before you apply.

Do you work with every genre?

We’re interested in artists with a distinct point of view, not a single genre label. Share your music and influences in the application so we can understand the project properly.

Do I need a large following?

No specific follower count is required by this page. Tell us where you are now, what you’ve been working on and what you want to build next. Fit is about the project and the partnership, not a vanity metric alone.

Can you guarantee streams or viral results?

No. Music marketing can help make your story clearer and your activity more deliberate, but no one can honestly guarantee audience behaviour, streaming numbers or viral reach.

What happens after I apply?

We review the information you share and aim to respond within 3–5 business days. If it looks like a potential fit, the next step is a conversation about your goals, needs and possible scope.

How does pricing work?

The right scope depends on your project and the support you need. We will discuss the work, expectations and cost with you before anything is agreed, so you can decide whether it makes sense for you.

Does submitting an application commit me to anything?

No. The application is an introduction, not a commitment to buy a service or work together. Any scope, expectations and costs should be discussed and agreed before work begins.
