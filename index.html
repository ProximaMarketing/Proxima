THE BRIEFFictional artist · New single

One song. Several decisions.

The track is ready, but there’s no agreed story, content direction or release rhythm. The task is to decide what matters first — and make the available time and effort count.

Inside the campaign

Four decisions that help the work hang together.

01Find the signal

Start with the song itself. What feeling, detail or point of view makes it memorable? That becomes the thread connecting the visuals, the language and the audience the artist wants to reach.

OUTPUT / A clear creative direction
02Shape the release

Choose a timeline the artist can sustain: introduce the idea, build interest with a few purposeful moments, make release day easy to find, then give listeners a reason to return afterwards.

OUTPUT / A practical campaign timeline
03Make content work together

Turn the central idea into a small set of formats — perhaps a lyric story, a performance clip and a visual detail from the recording. Each piece should feel related without becoming the same post repeated.

OUTPUT / Repeatable content ideas
04Learn from the response

Review the signals available to the artist, such as saves, shares, watch time, replies and link clicks. They won’t explain everything, but they can help decide which idea to develop and which to drop.

OUTPUT / A more informed next step

What is a promotional post? It is one publication on one agreed PROXIMA-managed channel. The packages cover promotion on PROXIMA channels, not posting to your own artist accounts. Publishing the same creative on a second channel counts as another post.

What is included in an edit? Each edit is one finished short-form video made from footage and approved music you supply. Filming and sourcing original footage are not included. Captions, opening hooks and hashtags are prepared for the agreed posts.

Good questions

Before you
reach out.

Do you work with every genre?

Genre alone doesn’t determine fit. Send us your music and influences so we can understand the project on its own terms.

Do I need a large following?

No. A link to your music and a clear idea of what you want to develop are enough to start a conversation.

Can you guarantee streams or viral results?

No. We can plan and deliver the agreed work, but views, streams, followers and audience behaviour are never guaranteed.

What happens after I apply?

We aim to reply within 3–5 business days. If it feels like a fit, we’ll arrange a conversation. Applying is not a commitment.

Where will my music be promoted?

Packages cover posts on agreed PROXIMA-managed channels, not posts to your own artist accounts. We’ll show you the channels and confirm the schedule before you commit. One post on one channel counts as one promotional post.

What do I need to provide?

You provide your music, approved source footage and any relevant release information. Video-editing allowances cover finished clips made from the material you send; filming and original footage creation are not included.

How many artists do you work with?

We limit monthly spaces so each project gets proper attention. If we’re full, you can still apply for the next opening.

How does pricing work?

Each package has a fixed monthly price and a defined allowance of promotional posts and edited videos. We confirm the channels, schedule and deliverables before you commit.
