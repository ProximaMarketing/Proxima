Good questions

Before you
reach out.

Do you work with every genre?

We look for a distinct point of view, not a genre label. Share your music and influences so we can understand the project.

Do I need a large following?

No. Tell us where you are and where you want to go. Fit matters more than follower count alone.

Can you guarantee streams or viral results?

No. We can sharpen your story and plan, but nobody can promise streams, audience behaviour or virality.

What happens after I apply?

We aim to reply within 3–5 business days. If it feels like a fit, we’ll arrange a conversation. Applying is not a commitment.

How does pricing work?

Scope and cost depend on your needs. We’ll agree both before any work begins.
