Good questions

Before you
reach out.

Do I need a large following?

No. Send us your music and we’ll take it from there.

Are results guaranteed?

No. Views, streams, followers and virality can’t be guaranteed.

What happens after I apply?

We aim to reply within 3–5 business days. Applying is not a commitment.

Where will you post?

On agreed PROXIMA-managed channels, not your own accounts. We’ll confirm the channels before you commit.

What do I need to provide?

Your music, approved footage and any relevant release details. Filming isn’t included.

What does each package include?

The monthly post and video allowances are listed above. We confirm your schedule and deliverables before starting.
