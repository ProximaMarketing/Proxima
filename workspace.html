How the data is sourced

What this first version can and cannot retrieve.

Metricool — your connected accounts

Export your TikTok post report and import it here. Connected-account metrics can include views, likes, comments, shares, reach, completion, watch time and traffic source. This first version does not call Metricool live from the local file.

Spotify for Artists — each artist's own account

Ask artists to export the relevant audience and song-stat CSVs or share authorised access. Spotify for Artists offers listener segments and exportable reports, but those are not the same as public fan-like data.

TikTok Creative Center — public trends

Check hashtags, trending sounds, related videos, regional popularity and trendlines. Record the date and evidence link in Trend Radar.

Comparable acts and public videos

Use public video examples and published view/like/share counts as directional signals only. Do not infer private demographics or claim to know exactly what individual viewers like.
