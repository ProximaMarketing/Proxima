A clear explanation of the cookies and third-party technologies used when you visit the PROXIMA Marketing website.

Last updated: 8 November 2026

On this page What the site uses Third-party services If this changes Contact

1. What this website currently uses

At the date of this policy, the PROXIMA Marketing website does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies, use advertising pixels or run behavioural advertising trackers. The site uses ordinary page functionality such as navigation, form submission and visual effects.

Because no non-essential cookies are currently used by the website, there is no analytics or advertising cookie preference to switch on or off. This policy will be updated if that changes.

2. Third-party services

The site loads typefaces from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those font files, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser details and the requested resource. This is a third-party request rather than an advertising cookie set by PROXIMA. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request and security information as part of providing the hosting service. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Links to music and social platforms open third-party services. Those services may use their own cookies and tracking technologies under their own policies.

3. If this changes

If we add analytics, advertising, embedded media or other technologies that store information on or access information from your device, we will update this policy and provide the choices and consent mechanism required by law before any non-essential technology is used.

Strictly necessary technologies may be used where they are essential to provide a service you have requested. We will explain any material change to the technologies used on this website.
