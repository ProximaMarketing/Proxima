2. Information we collect

We collect information you choose to give us through the enquiry form, email or other direct messages. This may include:

  • Your name, artist or project name, email address and optional phone number.
  • Links to your Spotify, Instagram, TikTok or other music and social profiles.
  • Information about your music, current projects, goals, release plans and the support you are considering.
  • Messages, preferences and other information you choose to share when discussing a potential or active service.
  • For clients, campaign assets and working information needed to prepare and publish agreed promotional content.

When you visit the website, the hosting provider may also process technical information such as your IP address, browser, device, requested pages and request timestamps in its server and security logs.

3. How and why we use it

We use personal information only where we have a lawful basis to do so. Depending on the circumstances, this may include:

  • Responding to an application or enquiry: to assess what you need, contact you and take steps at your request before entering a service agreement. The lawful basis is generally steps taken at your request before a contract, or our legitimate interests in responding to genuine enquiries.
  • Providing an agreed service: to plan, prepare, edit, schedule and report on the promotional work you have commissioned. The lawful basis is performance of the contract.
  • Keeping business records and handling disputes: to maintain appropriate records, protect the service and meet legal obligations. The lawful basis may be legal obligation or legitimate interests.
  • Sending our own marketing: only where we have a lawful basis and any consent required by electronic-marketing law. You can opt out at any time.

Submitting an application does not sign you up to a mailing list and does not create a service contract.

4. Who receives your information?

We do not sell your enquiry information. We share it only where needed to run the website or provide the service. Current service providers include:

  • Google Apps Script and Google Sheets: when you submit the enquiry form, the details are sent to a Google Apps Script endpoint and recorded in a Google Sheet for the operator to review and respond to. The spreadsheet is used for internal enquiry review. Access should be limited to people authorised to manage PROXIMA enquiries and the associated Google account should be kept secure.
  • GitHub Pages / GitHub: the website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical hosting and security logs when you visit.
  • Google Fonts: the site requests its typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google to retrieve those font files.

We may also share information if required by law, to protect legal rights or where you ask us to share it as part of a campaign. If content is published on a public social platform, the content and any information included in it may be visible to the public and processed by that platform under its own terms and privacy policy.

5. How long we keep information

We keep information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected, including any legal, accounting or dispute-resolution requirements. Our intended retention periods are:

  • Unsuccessful or inactive applications: normally up to 12 months after the last meaningful contact, then deleted or securely anonymised unless a longer period is needed to resolve a query or meet a legal obligation.
  • Client and campaign records: while the service is active and normally for up to 12 months afterwards, unless the records are needed for a legal claim or other lawful purpose.
  • Invoices and financial records: for the period required by applicable tax and accounting rules.
  • Technical hosting logs: in line with the hosting provider’s own retention and security practices.

These are the retention periods PROXIMA intends to apply. Enquiry and campaign records are reviewed periodically and deleted or anonymised when they are no longer needed, subject to legal record-keeping requirements.

6. Your data protection rights

Depending on the lawful basis and circumstances, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, have inaccurate information corrected, request erasure or restriction, object to certain processing, or receive information in a portable format. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw that consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before it was withdrawn.

To exercise a right, contact the operator using the email address in section 8. We may need to confirm your identity before responding. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

We do not use enquiry information to make decisions about you solely by automated means that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

7. Security, international transfers and links

We take reasonable steps to protect information, restrict access to people who need it and use reputable providers for website hosting and form storage. No method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

Google and GitHub may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where a restricted transfer takes place, the operator must ensure that an applicable UK adequacy regulation or an appropriate safeguard is in place. You can email the operator to ask for further information about the safeguards relied on for a particular transfer. The operator reviews the service providers’ current transfer terms and safeguards as part of maintaining this notice.

This website may link to Spotify, Instagram, TikTok, Reddit and other external services. Those services operate independently and have their own privacy notices. This notice does not cover their processing once you leave our website.
